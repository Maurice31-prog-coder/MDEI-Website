WHO WE ARE

Building people.
Strengthening communities.

Mass Developmental Empowerment Initiative (MDEI) is a youth-focused development initiative committed to empowering young people with the knowledge, skills, opportunities and support they need to thrive.

We believe that when young people are equipped to discover their potential, develop relevant skills and access meaningful opportunities, they become powerful contributors to sustainable development in their communities.
