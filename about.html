ABOUT MDEI

Empowering people.
Strengthening communities.

Mass Developmental Empowerment Initiative is a youth-focused development initiative committed to helping young people discover their potential, develop relevant skills and access meaningful opportunities.

WHO WE ARE

Development begins with people.

Mass Developmental Empowerment Initiative (MDEI) is a youth-focused initiative dedicated to empowering young people with the knowledge, skills, opportunities and support they need to thrive.

We believe that young people are not simply beneficiaries of development. They are active participants, innovators, leaders and contributors to the development of their communities.

MDEI therefore creates and supports opportunities that encourage learning, personal development, skills acquisition, career readiness, entrepreneurship and meaningful community participation.

01
